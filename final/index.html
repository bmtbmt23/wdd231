MY STORY


Boxing Is More Than a Sport


I started boxing during one of the most difficult moments of my life. After the sudden death of my late husband, my life changed completely. I felt lost and as if I were walking through a very dark time.


During that difficult season, I held onto my faith and continued to pray. Then boxing came into my life.


At first, boxing was simply a way for me to exercise. Over time, it became much more. It gave me a place to release my emotions, challenge myself, and take one step forward when life felt difficult.


Boxing did not erase my grief, but it helped me find strength while I was going through it. It helped me reconnect with myself and reminded me that even during painful seasons, we can continue moving forward.


That is why I created Boxing for Fitness and Health. I want to show that boxing can be more than a sport or a workout. It can help us care for our bodies, strengthen our minds, build confidence, and move forward through difficult moments in life.
